ABOUT ME

Understanding models.
Expanding possibilities.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, supervised by Prof. Pravendra Singh.

My work sits at the intersection of generative AI, machine learning, and computer vision. I study diffusion models, machine unlearning, efficient generation, and spatio-temporal modeling.

Previously, I was a research intern at Samsung Research Institute, Bangalore, exploring efficient diffusion transformers and generative image editing for on-device AI. I received my M.Tech. from Jawaharlal Nehru University in 2022.
