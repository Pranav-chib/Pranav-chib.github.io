I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science
and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT)
Roorkee, India, under the supervision of Prof. Pravendra Singh.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **Generative AI,
Machine Learning, and Computer Vision**, with a particular focus
on diffusion models, machine unlearning, efficient generative
models, and spatio-temporal modeling.

Previously, I worked as a Research Intern at Samsung Research
Institute, Bangalore (SRIB), where I explored efficient diffusion
transformers and generative image editing for on-device AI
applications.

I received my M.Tech. in Computer Science and Technology from
the School of Computer and Systems Sciences, Jawaharlal Nehru
University (JNU), New Delhi, in 2022.

## Research Interests

- Generative AI and Diffusion Models

- Efficient Generative Models and Model Acceleration
- Generative Image Editing and Personalization
- Large Language Models and Vision-Language Models
- Trajectory Prediction and Imputation
- Autonomous Driving and Computer Vision
